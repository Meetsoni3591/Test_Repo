# Test Cases for T1.1 - Project Objectives

Okay, here are two detailed test cases, written from the perspective of a Senior QA Engineer, based on the provided Project Objectives. I've focused on two different, but important, aspects of the "user-friendly platform for online shopping" objective: Search Functionality and Registration/Login.

**Test Case 1: Search Functionality - Accuracy and Relevance**

*   **Title:** Search Functionality - Accurate and Relevant Results

*   **Description:** This test case verifies that the search functionality returns accurate and relevant results based on various search queries. It focuses on the ability of the search engine to correctly identify and display products matching the user's search terms, handling variations in spelling, and filtering out irrelevant items.  This directly contributes to the "user-friendly" aspect by ensuring users can easily find what they are looking for.

*   **Steps:**

    1.  Navigate to the homepage of the online shopping platform.
    2.  Locate the search bar.
    3.  **Test Case 1.1: Exact Match Search:** Enter a product name that exists in the catalog (e.g., "Red Leather Jacket").
    4.  Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".
    5.  **Test Case 1.2: Partial Match Search:** Enter a partial product name (e.g., "Leather Jacket").
    6.  Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".
    7.  **Test Case 1.3: Typographical Error Search:** Enter a product name with a common spelling mistake (e.g., "Red Lether Jacket").
    8.  Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".
    9.  **Test Case 1.4: Synonym Search:** Enter a synonym for a product (e.g., search "shoes" when the product is named "footwear").
    10. Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".
    11. **Test Case 1.5: No Results Search:** Enter a non-existent product name (e.g., "Purple Unicorn Slippers").
    12. Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".
    13. **Test Case 1.6: Search by Category:** Enter a Category (e.g. "Electronics")
    14. Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".
    15. **Test Case 1.7: Search by Brand:** Enter a Brand (e.g. "Nike")
    16. Click the "Search" button or press "Enter".

*   **Expected Result:**

    *   **Result 1.1:** A list of products containing "Red Leather Jacket" in the title or description is displayed. The products displayed should be relevant to the search query.
    *   **Result 1.2:** A list of products containing "Leather Jacket" in the title or description is displayed. The results should be ranked by relevance, with the most relevant items appearing first.
    *   **Result 1.3:**  Either:
        *   A list of products containing "Red Leather Jacket" in the title or description is displayed (the platform autocorrects the query). OR
        *   A "Did you mean 'Red Leather Jacket'?" suggestion is displayed, and clicking the suggestion leads to the correct search results.
    *   **Result 1.4:** A list of products containing "footwear" in the title or description is displayed.
    *   **Result 1.5:** A message indicating that no products match the search criteria is displayed (e.g., "No results found"). This message should be user-friendly and potentially offer alternative suggestions (e.g., "Check your spelling" or "Try a different search term").
    *   **Result 1.6:** A list of products belonging to the "Electronics" category is displayed.
    *   **Result 1.7:** A list of products belonging to the "Nike" brand is displayed.

*   **Priority:** High

**Test Case 2: User Registration and Login - Usability and Security**

*   **Title:** User Registration and Login - Usability and Security

*   **Description:** This test case focuses on the registration and login process, ensuring it is both user-friendly and secure.  It verifies the ease of account creation, password requirements, error handling, and successful login functionality. A smooth and secure login process is essential for a positive user experience, and directly contributes to the "user-friendly" objective.

*   **Steps:**

    1.  Navigate to the registration page of the online shopping platform.
    2.  **Test Case 2.1: Valid Registration:** Enter a valid email address, strong password (including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters), and other required information.
    3.  Click the "Register" or "Create Account" button.
    4.  **Test Case 2.2: Invalid Email Format:** Enter an invalid email address (e.g., "test@test").
    5.  Enter a valid password and other required information.
    6.  Click the "Register" or "Create Account" button.
    7.  **Test Case 2.3: Weak Password:** Enter a weak password (e.g., "password").
    8.  Enter a valid email address and other required information.
    9.  Click the "Register" or "Create Account" button.
    10. **Test Case 2.4: Existing Email Address:** Enter an email address that is already registered.
    11. Enter a valid password and other required information.
    12. Click the "Register" or "Create Account" button.
    13. **Test Case 2.5: Successful Login:** After successful registration, navigate to the login page.
    14. Enter the registered email address and password.
    15. Click the "Login" button.
    16. **Test Case 2.6: Incorrect Password:** Enter the registered email address and an incorrect password.
    17. Click the "Login" button.
    18. **Test Case 2.7: Account Lockout:**  Attempt to log in with incorrect password multiple times (e.g. 5 times).
    19. **Test Case 2.8: "Forgot Password" functionality:** Click on the "Forgot Password" link, enter a valid email address and click submit.
    20. **Test Case 2.9: Check Reset Password email:** Check for an email containing a password reset link.

*   **Expected Result:**

    *   **Result 2.1:** The user is successfully registered and redirected to their account dashboard or a welcome page. A confirmation email should be sent to the registered email address (if implemented).
    *   **Result 2.2:** An error message is displayed indicating that the email address format is invalid. The registration form should remain populated with the entered (invalid) email address, allowing the user to correct it without re-entering other information.
    *   **Result 2.3:** An error message is displayed indicating that the password is too weak and must meet specific criteria (e.g., minimum length, inclusion of uppercase/lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters). The registration form should remain populated with the entered (weak) password, allowing the user to correct it.
    *   **Result 2.4:** An error message is displayed indicating that the email address is already registered. The registration form should remain populated with the entered email address, prompting the user to log in or use the "Forgot Password" functionality.
    *   **Result 2.5:** The user is successfully logged in and redirected to their account dashboard or the homepage.
    *   **Result 2.6:** An error message is displayed indicating that the email address or password is incorrect.  A "Forgot Password" link should be prominently displayed.
    *   **Result 2.7:** The user is temporarily locked out of their account and a message is displayed indicating this lockout, along with instructions on how to unlock the account (e.g., wait a certain amount of time or contact support).
    *   **Result 2.8:** An email is sent to the specified email address with instructions on how to reset the password.
    *   **Result 2.9:** The email is successfully received, contains a clear and working password reset link, and doesn't contain any broken HTML or formatting issues.

*   **Priority:** High

These test cases are a starting point and can be expanded upon to cover more edge cases and specific requirements of the online shopping platform. Good luck with your testing!
