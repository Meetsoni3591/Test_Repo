# Test Cases for T8.2 - Integration with Third-Party Tools

**Test Case 1: Payment Gateway Integration - Successful Transaction**

*   **Title:** Payment Gateway Integration - Successful Credit Card Transaction

*   **Description:** Verifies that the system correctly processes a successful credit card transaction through the integrated payment gateway. This includes initiating the payment request, correctly transmitting payment information to the gateway, receiving a successful authorization response, and updating the system's records accordingly. This test case is crucial for ensuring revenue generation and maintaining customer trust.

*   **Steps:**

    1.  **Pre-Condition:** A valid user account exists in the system. The system is connected to a configured payment gateway in a test environment. The payment gateway test account has sufficient funds for the transaction. Valid credit card information (card number, expiration date, CVV) for the test environment is available.
    2.  Log in to the application as a valid user.
    3.  Navigate to the checkout page or the page where payment is initiated.
    4.  Select the "Credit Card" payment option.
    5.  Enter the valid credit card information (card number, expiration date, CVV) and billing address.
    6.  Initiate the payment process by clicking the "Submit Payment" or similar button.
    7.  Monitor the system's progress indicators or loading screens during the payment processing time.
    8.  Observe the system's response after the payment is submitted.
    9.  Verify that a "Payment Successful" message is displayed to the user.
    10. Navigate to the order history or transaction details page.
    11. Verify that the order status is updated to "Paid" or a similar status indicating successful payment.
    12. Access the payment gateway's test environment dashboard or transaction logs.
    13. Confirm that the transaction appears in the payment gateway's records with the correct amount, status (e.g., "Authorized," "Captured"), and transaction details.
    14. Review the system logs for any errors, warnings, or unexpected messages during the payment processing flow.

*   **Expected Result:**

    *   The payment gateway successfully authorizes the credit card transaction.
    *   The user receives a confirmation message indicating successful payment.
    *   The order status is updated to reflect successful payment within the system.
    *   The transaction details are accurately recorded in both the system and the payment gateway's records (amount, currency, transaction ID, etc.).
    *   No errors or warnings are logged in the system during the payment process.
    *   The system is not in a stuck state after a completed transaction.

*   **Priority:** High (Critical for core functionality)

**Test Case 2: Email Marketing Tool Integration - Subscription Confirmation**

*   **Title:** Email Marketing Tool Integration - Successful Subscription Confirmation

*   **Description:** Validates that when a user subscribes through the application, their email address is correctly added to the designated mailing list within the integrated email marketing tool, and a confirmation email is successfully sent to the user.  This ensures accurate contact list management and effective communication with subscribers. This test case is important for growth and engagement with customers.

*   **Steps:**

    1.  **Pre-Condition:** The system is integrated with a configured email marketing tool. A designated mailing list exists in the email marketing tool. A valid email address for testing purposes is available. The email marketing tool's settings allow for subscription confirmation emails.
    2.  Navigate to the subscription form or signup page on the application.
    3.  Enter the valid test email address into the email input field.
    4.  Provide any other required information (e.g., name, preferences) if necessary.
    5.  Submit the subscription form by clicking the "Subscribe" or similar button.
    6.  Observe the system's response after submitting the form (e.g., a confirmation message displayed on the page).
    7.  Check the inbox of the test email address.
    8.  Verify that a subscription confirmation email is received from the system or the email marketing tool.
    9.  Open the confirmation email and review its content.
    10. Click the confirmation link within the email (if applicable).
    11. Observe the page the confirmation link directs to and the message displayed.
    12. Log in to the email marketing tool's dashboard.
    13. Navigate to the designated mailing list.
    14. Verify that the test email address has been successfully added to the mailing list and that the status is "Confirmed" or similar.
    15. Review the system logs for any errors, warnings, or unexpected messages during the subscription process.

*   **Expected Result:**

    *   The test email address is successfully added to the designated mailing list in the email marketing tool.
    *   The status of the email address in the mailing list is "Confirmed" after clicking the confirmation link (if applicable).
    *   A subscription confirmation email is successfully sent to the test email address.
    *   The confirmation email contains relevant information about the subscription and instructions (if any).
    *   The confirmation link (if applicable) directs the user to a confirmation page with a success message.
    *   No errors or warnings are logged in the system during the subscription or confirmation process.

*   **Priority:** Medium (Important for marketing and customer communication)
